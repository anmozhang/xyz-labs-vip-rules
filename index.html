Max loss is static: a fixed dollar amount based on the starting account size. It does not trail upward as your balance grows. Example: a Starter account's max-loss floor stays at $4,700 ($5,000 − $300), no matter how much profit you make.
Daily loss is EOD-based: the daily limit is measured from your end-of-day (EOD) balance and resets at each day's close. Example: if a Starter account closed yesterday at $5,200, today's daily-loss floor is $5,050 ($5,200 − $150).

FAQ

  1. What if I fail once on X / Y Mode?Path A requires zero failed accounts during the period, so a fail means you don't meet this path.
  2. I had 2 payouts on Z Mode, then missed one?The streak resets to zero and you start again from the first payout.
  3. My VIP account failed this month. Do I still pay the monthly fee?Yes. VIP is a subscription charged every month ($89 / $229 / $429 depending on tier), regardless of whether the account failed.
  4. Can I reset a VIP account after it fails?Yes. A reset costs an extra $89, on top of the monthly subscription.
  5. How are VIP profits split?You get a 60% profit split.
  6. What are the VIP tiers?Starter ($5k, $89/month), Pro ($15k, $229/month) and Whale ($30k, $429/month). All tiers use 2x max leverage, a 60% split and payouts every 7 days.
  7. Can I still buy X, Y or Z Mode after joining VIP?No. If you select the VIP Project, you will no longer be able to purchase X, Y, or Z modes.
  8. How do I get the consolation prize?Lose 3 accounts in a row and you receive 1 free account.
